# Vacuquest: Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 1, 2026

Vacuquest is a game for Android phones and tablets and for iPhone and iPad, made by Matanya Asis, trading as As Is ("we", "us"). Contact: matanya.asis288@gmail.com.

The short version: Vacuquest doesn't collect, store or share any personal information. There are no ads, no analytics and no tracking, and you don't need an account.

## What the game keeps

- **Your game progress.** Rooms cleared, upgrades and settings are saved on your device only. If device backup is on (Android backup with your Google account, or iCloud backup on iPhone and iPad), it may include this small file. That backup is handled by Google or Apple under your account, not by us.
- **No account with us.** We don't ask for your email, name or any other personal details.

## Purchases

The first room is free. The rest of the game is a one-time in-app purchase, handled entirely by Google Play on Android or the App Store on iPhone and iPad. We never see your payment details. Your purchase is covered by the Google Privacy Policy (https://policies.google.com/privacy) or Apple's Privacy Policy (https://www.apple.com/legal/privacy/). The game only checks with Google Play or the App Store whether the unlock was bought.

## Ads, analytics and tracking

None. The game shows no ads, uses no advertising ID, and sends no usage or crash data to us or to anyone else.

## Children

Vacuquest doesn't collect personal information from anyone, children included.

## Changes

If this policy changes, the new version will be posted here with a new "Last updated" date.
